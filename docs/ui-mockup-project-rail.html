Project settings

Per-project engine override
vibeflow-hermes-execution
sport-host

Concept

Paper card index — projects are quiet tabbed dividers; conversations filed under them. Rejected: bold caps + folder emoji reflex.

Divider is quieter than the entries (0.68rem vs 0.78rem). Only Ideas tints amber.

Grouping rules

Groups ordered by newest entry (sort_updated_at desc), ties by name; Ideas always last (catch-all, not peer).

Sessions keep catalog order inside each group — the group never re-sorts entries.

Suggestion chip

Appears in composer area after a message when classifier proposes a project. Never auto-moves on low confidence.

Two variants: → Move to <project>? and “Tạo project mới <tên>?” when no match.

Keyboard: Tab reaches chip, Esc dismisses, Enter on button acts.

A11y

Group = section[aria-label]; chip = role=status (announceable); all contrast pairs computed (WCAG AA ≥4.5:1, dark-mode pairs included).

Known limitations

repo_root defaults to cwd (never empty) → Unassigned/Ideas is the rare tail, not the primary case.

Same-basename clones merge into one group visually (named-project identity is the future upgrade).

Interactive

Click a divider to collapse/expand its entries. Click “Move”. Esc on the chip dismisses.
